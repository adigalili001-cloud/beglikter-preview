The artist · b. 1977 · Tel Aviv

Etamar Beglikter

[Sculptor. Cuts the Hebrew Encyclopedia — the pride of every Israeli living room — back into the words it was meant to hold.]

[Works with encyclopedia volumes, PVC and ready-made objects. Playful on the surface; underneath, loss, memory and what a culture chooses to keep.]
