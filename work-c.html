Brainless

bli moach · בלי מוח

Two heads, cut from the encyclopedia that was supposed to fill them.

[The Hebrew Encyclopedia was the pride of every Israeli living room. Today the sets are left on sidewalks, beside the recycling bins.]

[Etamar cut the two volumes that hold the entry for “brain” until only two profiles remained. Nothing was added — the rest of the knowledge is still there, in the layers of the edge.]

2008
Hand-cut volumes of the Hebrew Encyclopedia
31 × 46 × 7 cm
