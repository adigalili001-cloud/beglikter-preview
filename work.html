The story+
Two heads, cut from the encyclopedia that was supposed to fill them. The Hebrew Encyclopedia was the pride of every Israeli living room; today the sets are left on sidewalks. Etamar cuts each volume down until only one shape remains — the rest of the knowledge is still there, in the layers of the edge.
